- The Guardian's storage-pool alert no longer pages on every crossing of a tier line. Like the RAM alert, a tier is left only once the reading is `storage_pool.exit_margin_pct` points (default 5) below its line, tracked per axis; severity, the exhaustion warning and the autonomous provisioning offer follow the measured reading, and a page sent while the margin holds the tier says so. RAM recovery pages no longer say "pool recovered".

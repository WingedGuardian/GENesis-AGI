- Memory recall no longer turns a long pasted prompt into a full-text query over every word in it. The expanded query, the file-keyword lane's base and the AND→OR retry now keep at most 32 distinct terms (the most frequent in the prompt), and prompts within that budget are unchanged. Measured on a copy of a 113,000-row memory index over 40 long and 20 short real prompts: the slowest 5% of long-prompt searches fell from 26 s to 0.75 s and the largest temp sort from 155 MiB to 9 MiB; short prompts returned identical results. On long prompts the top results change (median 29% overlap with the unbounded ranking). Query expansion also skips the `session_note` tag, which sits on most memories and widened every expanded query.

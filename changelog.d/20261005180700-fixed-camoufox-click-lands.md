- **Browser clicks land, or fail saying why.** On the default Camoufox browser, a click on an element below the visible part of the page used to do nothing while reporting success, and a click on an element hidden behind a cookie banner or dialog clicked the banner instead. Clicks now scroll the element into view and aim at a point that is really on the element (a link that wraps over two lines or a rotated element no longer counts as covered by its own container). If something covers the element, the click fails with an error naming the covering element, so it can be dismissed and the click retried, and no fallback presses Enter behind it. A styled checkbox or radio is clicked through its label. A click that may already have been delivered is never repeated.

- Past the terminal review round, an agent can merge a pull request only at that round's head, or at that head plus empty commits. If the head moved after it (a fix commit, a merge of main or a force-push), the merge is blocked, and the owner either merges it themselves or sends it back. An unreadable review budget now blocks every agent merge, at any round count, and `--check-pr` shows the check in a new blocking `terminal-head` row.

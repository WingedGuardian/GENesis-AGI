- User-job runs now remain running until their dispatched session ends, then record the session outcome and reason. Startup reconciliation recovers interrupted runs, and post-spawn bookkeeping errors no longer turn a successful dispatch into a job failure. A job's status, result and failure count are now recomputed from its runs, so when runs overlap an older run's completion no longer overwrites the newest run's status and every failure is counted; a job left marked running by an interrupted completion is repaired at startup.

- A validator session can now record what it concluded about a merged PR, and the ledger keeps the reason when it could not finish. Four verdicts: two of them close the obligation, and `fail-intent` / `cannot-verify` leave it open with a note the next validator inherits — so a PR that could not be checked on your install says why, instead of looking untouched. `scripts/pr_verification.py close --pr N --verdict …` writes it; `repo_pulse_worker.py --verification-backlog` shows attempted rows inline and `--verification-log [--pr N]` shows what was decided and on what evidence. A verdict is not taken on the validator's word: the evidence document names the ledger row it verifies, and a closing verdict is refused unless at least one claim is both passing and measured or read — so an obligation cannot be marked handled by a document that established nothing.

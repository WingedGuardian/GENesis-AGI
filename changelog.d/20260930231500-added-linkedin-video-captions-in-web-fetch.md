- **`web_fetch` now reads what a LinkedIn post's video says.** For a LinkedIn post, the page is fetched as before, and when the post has a captioned video its transcript is appended under a `## Video transcript` heading, so the inbox evaluator (which has no shell) sees the video's content, not just the post text. It uses captions only, never audio; a post whose video has no captions says so in `video_error`, and a text-only post is unchanged. Knowledge ingestion now also recognises every YouTube video URL shape (`/live/`, `/embed/`, mobile and music hosts).

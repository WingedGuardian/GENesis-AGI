- **The morning report no longer presents an old observation's figure as the current state, or restates a cause from an observation as fact.** Its prompt now treats every figure inside an observation (a level, a peak, or a count over a time window) as measured when that observation was written: stated with its time or window, never as the present level, and never as a blocker unless live state in the report shows the same condition. A cause that appears in an observation is attributed to that observation or omitted, never restated as settled.

- **The tmp watchgod now guards whole disks instead of policing a 500 MB budget, and never kills a session or deletes live work.** It watches every filesystem Genesis writes to (root, `$HOME`, `/tmp`, the cc-tmp volume), including a separately limited volume that shares a device with root, and measures the space that is really left — whichever is closer of what `df` reports and the btrfs quota on the volume, plus btrfs metadata headroom, which `df` cannot see. Tiers follow free space and time-to-full: YELLOW logs who is writing, ORANGE starts a reclaim pass (caches, `~/tmp` older than 2 days) and pages a warning, RED releases a preallocated reserve file, freezes a runaway downloader (curl, wget, yt-dlp and similar) writing into `~/tmp/downloads` — paused, not killed, and only once a record `thaw` can find has been written — and pages. Reclaim runs (daily and under pressure) never overlap, and uninstall stops them. `scripts/watchgod status` shows the state; `scripts/watchgod thaw <pid>` resumes a frozen download. cc-tmp gets real retention instead: an hourly sweep removes what ended sessions left behind once nothing holds it and nothing has touched it for 7 days. Install-local overrides (observe mode `WATCHGOD_ACT=0`, thresholds) go in `~/.genesis/config/watchgod.local.conf`. The old `CC_TMP_BUDGET_MB`, `SACRED_GROUND_MB` and `CC_TMP_CAPACITY_MB` settings no longer do anything.

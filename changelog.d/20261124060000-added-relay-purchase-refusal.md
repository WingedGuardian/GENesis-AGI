- **A relayed session cannot click through a purchase.** A session answering a same-owner peer that holds `owner_relay` now has its browser clicks, its submitting key presses (Enter or Space) and its form fills checked: a control whose label reads as a purchase (pay, buy, place order, checkout, subscribe and similar) is refused, and so is a line break typed into a field whose form's button reads as a purchase. It is best effort: it refuses only on a clear match and allows whatever it cannot read, so ordinary clicks and form filling keep working. Only the owner can buy. Sessions without a relaying peer are unchanged.

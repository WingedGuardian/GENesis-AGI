- **A relayed session cannot click through a purchase.** A session answering a same-owner peer that holds `owner_relay` now has its browser clicks, its submitting key presses (Enter or Space) and its form fills checked: a control whose label reads as a purchase (pay, buy, place order, checkout, subscribe and similar) is refused, and so is a line break typed into a single-line field, where it can submit the form. Only the owner can buy. Sessions without a relaying peer are unchanged.

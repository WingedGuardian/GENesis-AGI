- A root Tailscale watchdog (`genesis-tailscale-watchdog.timer`, every ~2 min) heals a stuck Tailscale tunnel: when a peer that traffic wants has not completed a WireGuard handshake for 5 minutes, and the peer answers discovery pings while the tunnel does not, it runs `systemctl try-restart tailscaled`, at most once an hour. What the restart did is read back from systemd, and it counts as a heal only if the stuck peer then answers through the tunnel. Each outcome becomes an owner alert through the awareness tick: a heal is high priority, and a restart that failed, did not clear the tunnel, or could not be confirmed pages once per incident. A watchdog that stops reporting, or keeps reporting that it cannot check any tunnel, raises its own alert, and the protection-posture check flags a host with tailscaled but no enabled watchdog. It installs on any systemd host with a tailscaled unit. `NETWD_TS_MODE=observe|off` (a drop-in) or masking the timer turns it down or off, and `scripts/uninstall.sh` now removes both root watchdog timers.

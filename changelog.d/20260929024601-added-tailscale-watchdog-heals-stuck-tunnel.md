- A root Tailscale watchdog (`genesis-tailscale-watchdog.timer`, every ~2 min) heals a stuck Tailscale tunnel: when a peer that traffic wants has not completed a WireGuard handshake for 5 minutes, and the peer answers discovery pings while the tunnel does not, it runs `systemctl try-restart tailscaled`, at most once an hour. What the restart did is read back from systemd, and it counts as a heal only if the stuck peer then answers through the tunnel. Each run reports only what it observed, per peer; the awareness tick keeps one owner alert per stuck peer, paged once and resolved only on evidence that the tunnel answers again or the peer went offline, and only peers confirmed stuck in the same run are ever restarted; a restart that failed, hung or could not be confirmed pages too. A watchdog that stops reporting, or keeps reporting that it cannot check any tunnel, raises its own alert, and the protection-posture check flags a host with tailscaled but no enabled watchdog. It installs on any systemd host with a tailscaled unit. `NETWD_TS_MODE=observe|off` (a drop-in) or masking the timer turns it down or off, and `scripts/uninstall.sh` now removes both root watchdog timers.

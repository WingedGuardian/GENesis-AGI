- A merge through the merge gate that carries the `--body-file` from `--check-pr` now leaves a receipt in the squash commit: `Gate-*` trailers record each blocking gate's own outcome (`ok`, `n/a`, `unverified` or `waived`), a verdict derived from them (`PASSED`, `PASSED-UNVERIFIED` or `OVERRIDDEN`), the override sigils, any stand-in reviewer, and the merging session and install ids. `--check-pr` prints that command in a new `body-file` row, even when other rows block. A merge under an override sigil must carry the file whenever the gate can produce it, and its refusal prints the command; a plain merge may leave it off. A missing receipt means no gate record, not a bypass.

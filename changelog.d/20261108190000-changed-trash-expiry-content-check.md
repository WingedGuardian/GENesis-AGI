- **`--expire-trash` now deletes a worktree archive only when its content is proven to be in `main`.** Before, the manual expiry trusted metadata the reaper recorded at archive time, which could miss ignored files, edits made after the dirty check, and work matched only by patch id. Now an archive expires only when its recorded head is an ancestor of `main` or the exact head of a merged PR, and every file in it matches that head byte for byte or is a regenerable cache or generated file. Anything else, including a corrupt archive or a failed check, keeps the archive and logs why. A dry run reports how many would expire. Nothing schedules expiry yet.

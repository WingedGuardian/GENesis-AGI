- **Round reflections can carry a prediction, and past round 4 they will have to.** The reflection grammar accepts `Prediction:`, `Confidence:` and `Prediction-held:` lines in any round. A new rule set holds a fix-now past round 3 to a prediction with high confidence, checks the held judgement from round 5, leaves only a send-back after two failed predictions, and refuses a fix-now for a round 5 or later that drew no floor finding. Rounds reached by a merge of main, an empty commit or a force-pushed-away head are left out.

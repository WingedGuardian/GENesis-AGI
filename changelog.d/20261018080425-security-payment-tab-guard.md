- **A browser tab can now be armed as a payment tab, ready for the card-fill flow (#3172).** No caller arms one yet. While a tab is armed, its page snapshots hide every text field, number field and dropdown value. Text on any page that matches a filled value (by keyed hash; the values themselves are never kept) is hidden too. `browser_run_js` is refused in the whole browser. The armed tab refuses screenshots, typing, uploads and navigation. `browser_press_key` takes only focus, activation and scroll keys there, and a click on it reports URLs without their query strings and errors as fixed text. Log lines keep their text but drop their arguments and tracebacks, and the Turnstile debug log writes nothing. The tab's form fields draw no visible text on screen, including after Back. The payment flow ends by closing the tab and clearing its sites' local storage, IndexedDB and caches. Any `browser_run_js`, or any navigation to a non-http(s) URL, since Camoufox started marks the browser as unfit for card entry until it restarts. With `card_fill.enabled` set in `config/payments.yaml` (off by default; `GENESIS_CARD_FILL_DISABLED=1` wins), Camoufox launches with form history, autofill, session-store form data, closed-tab undo and select-to-copy turned off.

- **Steering rules now reach every session through a session-start part of their own.** The hook used to carry SOUL.md and STEERING.md in one part with a 9,800-character budget, which left room for about five captured rules before the newest were truncated. It is now five hook entries: `charter`, `soul`, `steering`, `identity-user` and `knowledge`, so the captured rules in `STEERING.local.md` get the whole steering budget. The soul and steering entries are wired as `--part identity-core --only <part>`. A session whose wiring predates this update still invokes `identity-core` (after a compaction, say), which still carries both files. And a session whose wiring is newer than the script it runs (a worktree before the main checkout deploys, or after a rollback) gets SOUL and STEERING twice, within budget, instead of losing both.

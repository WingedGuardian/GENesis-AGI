- **Browser clicks land, or fail saying why.** On the default Camoufox browser, a click on an element below the visible part of the page used to do nothing while reporting success, and a click on an element hidden behind a cookie banner or dialog clicked the banner instead. Clicks now scroll the element into view and aim at a point that is really on the element (a link that wraps over two lines or a rotated element no longer counts as covered by its own container). If something covers the element, the click fails with an error naming the covering element, so it can be dismissed and the click retried, and no fallback presses Enter behind it. A styled checkbox or radio is clicked through its label. A click that may already have been delivered is never repeated.
- **Driving your own Chrome (remote CDP) uses a tab of its own.** Genesis now opens one tab in your Chrome and works there, instead of taking over the first tab you had open. It reuses that tab when it reconnects and never closes it. A remote session no longer switches on fast "collaborate" timing for the built-in browser that runs afterwards (remote actions keep their own fast timing). `browser_snapshot` on a remote tab now clears the "page state changed" advisory, as the advisory says it will.
- **Long form fields no longer time out.** `browser_fill` had a deadline based on the length of the text, which cut off values of a few hundred characters and reset the page. It now fails only if the browser stops responding for 30 seconds in one step.
- **`browser_sessions` and `browser_clear_domain` cover both browser profiles.** They now read and clear Camoufox's cookies as well as Chromium's, label each result by browser, and use the running browser when one is open. Clearing `x.com` no longer also clears `netflix.com`: domains match whole labels (`x.com` and its subdomains). `browser_clear_domain` returns the number of cookies removed instead of yes or no.
- The browser tools' shared log (`~/tmp/mcp_health.log`) is now rotated by the daily disk-hygiene job once it passes 10 MB, keeping two older copies; it previously grew without limit.

- **The Guardian now keeps its own snapshots from filling an LVM-thin storage pool, instead of only warning about it.** A thin snapshot costs almost nothing when it is taken, then grows with every block the container rewrites. The daily "healthy" rollback snapshot is replaced create-first, and the replacement can be refused once the pool is past LVM's autoextend threshold. When that happened, the old snapshot stayed and kept growing. On one install it grew for a week until the pool hit 100% and the container's filesystem went read-only, while CRITICAL alerts fired every six hours. (An earlier entry said this snapshot is "never more than a day old"; that held only while the replacement succeeded.) Now:
  - When the pool refuses the new snapshot and the measured growth says the pool really is under pressure, the Guardian deletes the stale one first, then creates it. A pool that is full but barely growing keeps its existing rollback snapshot, since deleting it there would free almost nothing. A refused refresh retries within about an hour and sends an alert, throttled rather than hourly.
  - Every tick, the Guardian measures how fast the pool is actually filling and frees space it owns before it fills. Within ~48h of full, it removes pre-recovery snapshots and a rollback snapshot older than 48 hours. Within ~24h of full, or when free space drops under a burst reserve, it removes any Guardian snapshot, the rollback snapshot last. It frees one item at a time, waits for a fresh measurement before the next, and alerts on each.
  - On LVM pools that already carry Genesis's `genesis-thinpool` autoextend profile, it first grows the pool into free volume-group space. LVM's own autoextend can leave that space unused, because it refuses when less than a full 20% step is free.
  - Relief only deletes snapshots with the exact timestamped names the Guardian generates itself (`guardian-YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS…`), never one you created by hand. A pool that is not under pressure keeps its rollback snapshot, however long an outage lasts.
  - Levers: `storage_pool.relief_mode` (`live` / `alert_only` / `off`) and the horizons in `guardian.yaml`, plus the `GUARDIAN_POOL_RELIEF_DISABLED=1` kill switch, which stops every automatic delete and extend. Recovery runbook: `docs/reference/thin-pool-recovery.md`.

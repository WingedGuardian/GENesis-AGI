- An update that dies after stopping `genesis-server` (a crash, a kill or a power loss, at any point after the stop) no longer leaves the server down: the next update restarts and health-checks it, even when there is nothing new to merge, when `genesis-bridge` is running in its place, or after the update-failure alert has been raised and archived. An update that cannot record the restart it would owe refuses to stop the server. A server stopped by the operator, and not by an update, still stays stopped, including one stopped after something else had already restarted it. Bootstrap's crash recovery reports such a crash as an update failure on the dashboard.

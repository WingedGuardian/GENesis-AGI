- **Reviewers are told to put leak, destructive and fail-open findings at the blocking level.** The merge gate's always-fix floor reads a finding's severity label, not what it describes. Over the 80 most recently merged and 94 open pull requests, 66 of 459 findings labelled below the floor described one of these three kinds. The review mandate in `AGENTS.md`, a new `REVIEW.md` and CodeRabbit's path instructions now tell each reviewer to label such a finding Codex P1, CodeRabbit Critical or Devin severe, and to put a `Kind:` line right after its severity header. The gate itself is unchanged: whether it should also read that line is decided by measuring the next 40 findings of these kinds.

- **The merge gate now scores Devin's review findings like Codex's.** A Devin severe bug or critical security finding blocks a merge in every lane, and a non-severe one counts toward the review score like a Codex P2; a finding Devin posted twice counts once, and Devin can withdraw its own finding with a `✅ **Resolved**` reply. CodeRabbit scoring is unchanged. When Codex has not reviewed a PR's head, `# substitute-review` lets a Devin or CodeRabbit review of that exact head stand in for it — the gate asks the owner to approve each time, refuses in unattended sessions, and keeps the base-branch check and the head binding that `# stale-review-override` drops.

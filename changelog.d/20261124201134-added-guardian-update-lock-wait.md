- **With the `live` switch on, the Guardian never recovers into a running deploy.** With `recovery.live_engine: true` and the container checkout on `live`, no recovery action runs while `update.lock` is held, or while it cannot be read and the container is not reported stopped: the check runs when the approval flow starts, again after the investigation, and once more just before an approved action (and after its snapshot). The server restart is queued under the lock without holding it across the start. A wait where nothing ran costs no recovery attempt. While it waits, the Guardian reads the lock on every check and diagnoses again as soon as it is free (or the container is stopped); a lock still held is reported every 2 h and nothing is restarted, and a lock that cannot be read on a running container is reported, at once and then every 2 h, as an approval to restart the container. A lock held at any path the Guardian can find reads held even when another path could not be checked. An approval that its recovery backoff holds is kept and acted on after the backoff, never asked again; one that arrives after the approval window, or once the checkout has left `live`, is not acted on, and the owner is told. Each wait event sends one message, not two. The alerts include what the host can see of the lock's holder, as a hint. With the switch off (the default) nothing changes.

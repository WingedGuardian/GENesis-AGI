- **Driving your own Chrome (remote CDP) uses a tab of its own.** Genesis now opens one tab in your Chrome and works there, instead of taking over the first tab you had open. It reuses that tab when it reconnects and never closes it. A remote session no longer switches on fast "collaborate" timing for the built-in browser that runs afterwards (remote actions keep their own fast timing). `browser_snapshot` on a remote tab now clears the "page state changed" advisory, as the advisory says it will.

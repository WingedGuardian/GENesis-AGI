- When a browser click, or a fill's focus click, fails after Playwright has started sending the mouse events ("performing click action"), the error now carries a `diagnostics` snapshot taken at that moment: the browser layer and its age, open pages, the document's visibility, focus and ready state, the last JS dialog, and the target's box, hit-test, enabled and pointer-events state. Elements are named by standard tag and ARIA role only, and any other value shows as `<unlisted>` or `<custom>`; no field value or page text is included. If the page navigated after the click, the snapshot says so instead of describing the new page, and a click that fails close to its 60-second limit skips the probes rather than losing its error.

- When the review escalation gate blocks at either of its two escalation tiers (the round-2 mode switch and the round-3 cap), the remedies it offers are now put to you directly. Previously the gate printed its options and the session relayed them — which is how a real block once reached the user with the first option missing, a fourth invented, and "ship as-is" added. The gate's own question, with the gate's own wording and its options in the gate's own order, is now appended to the session's next question automatically, so the menu you answer is the one the gate wrote. Nothing else changes: `# escalation-ack` and `# audit-ack` mean exactly what they meant before, and no gate reads your answer back. The menu stops being offered once a commit on that branch goes through, so a decision you have already made is not put to you again. `python3 scripts/review_state.py gate-demand` shows the menu currently recorded for a branch. The final-round terminal — accept-and-merge versus abandon — is not covered and still relies on the session relaying its two options. Turn the substitution off with `GENESIS_GATE_ACK_DISABLED=1` or by creating `~/.genesis/config/gate_ack_disabled`; with it off, the gate blocks and asks exactly as it did before.

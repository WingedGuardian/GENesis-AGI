- **One-call transcript lookup on an edge device.** A new `edge_transcripts` tool lists a day's transcript files on the edge device configured in `ambient_remote.yaml`, or finds a phrase in them, in one call (status, list, or search with two lines of context). Nothing is copied into Genesis. Set `transcripts_dir` in `ambient_remote.yaml` to enable it; without it the tool reports "not configured".

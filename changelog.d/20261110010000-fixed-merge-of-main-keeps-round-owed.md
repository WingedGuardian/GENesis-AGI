- **Merging main into a PR branch no longer erases the latest review round's debt.** When nothing after the newest reviewed head is a fix (only merges of main, or empty commits such as a pushed reflection), the commit gate keeps that round open: its findings stay owed, and the round reflection names the reviewed head. A fix commit made after such a merge used to be admitted with nothing owed and no warning. The gate now also warns when a real commit has moved past a review round that never got a reflection.

- **Genesis can now answer questions from another AI agent you use.** A new connector lets an external personal-agent front-end ask your install something in plain language over your tailnet and get the answer back, so the agent you talk to day to day can hand the heavy thinking here instead of guessing. It is off until you set `GENESIS_AGENT_API_TOKEN` in `secrets.env` -- blank, whitespace or anything under 16 characters counts as unset, and the boot log tells you which state you are in. Publish it with a path-scoped tailnet listener rather than by opening the dashboard port, because that port also carries a route with no authentication of its own. The calling agent is treated as external throughout: it cannot change your model or effort setting by writing `/model` in a question, its conversations are barred from steering what Genesis learns about you, and long answers come back whole rather than chopped at a messaging-app length limit.

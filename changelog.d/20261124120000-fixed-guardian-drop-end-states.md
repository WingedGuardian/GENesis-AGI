- **A killed Guardian tick no longer loses what it was doing, and an approved recovery held back by its backoff still runs.** Before an approved action runs, the Guardian now counts it and saves its state to disk, recording a `live` drop as attempted before the engine call (a clean refusal puts back the record that was there before), and a SIGTERM from systemd still saves the state and stops the running `incus exec` client. A tick killed mid-action wakes as RECOVERED instead of staying "recovery in progress"; a gate wait killed at the unit's timeout keeps the episode's alert latch, so the episode stays quiet until the confirmed-dead auto-reset re-diagnoses it. An approved action deferred by a backoff of at most 900 s is waited out inside the tick and then run unless Genesis is back, with no second diagnosis or prompt, and a proposal whose own budget is spent (IO_TRIAGE's counter included) escalates instead of being put to the approval gates. These apply to every install. `deploy_candidates drop` and `rebuild` now remove a harmless leftover `update_state.json` only after their last refusal under the checkout lock, so a refusal that says nothing changed (a busy checkout lock, a refused manifest write) keeps it.

- **Past ordinary-lane round 4, the review loop now ends instead of running on.** It ends when the newest review round is answered by a send-back or with every finding filed as an issue (from round 4), or when a round from round 5 on leaves nothing at the always-fix floor (Codex P1, Devin severe, CodeRabbit Critical/Major). The commit gate then refuses further content commits on the PR; a merge of the default branch and an empty reflection commit still pass, and a new review round reopens fixing. A reflection that still fixes something past round 4 states which round it predicts the PR merges at, with a confidence of at least 95%. From round 5 each reflection says whether the last prediction held, checked against the review facts. Two rounds that drew floor findings leave send-back as the only decision. Rounds opened before the change keep the old rules.
